Event Planner Management in Odoo

Event Planner Management Odoo App turns Odoo into one central workspace to plan, coordinate, and deliver events. It is built for event planning agencies, wedding planners, corporate event teams, catering firms, and venue managers who run every event from the first inquiry through to the day itself and final billing.

Clients send inquiries from your website, receive an online quotation, e-sign it, and pay a booking deposit online. Once that quote is signed and paid, the event books itself: it opens a project with a checklist whose deadlines count back from the event date, such as 30 and 7 days before. From the event you source vendors and raise purchase orders, build a live budget of estimated, contracted, and actual cost, assign staff with double-booking checks, keep a guest list, and lay out a minute-by-minute run of show.

A live dashboard shows pipeline value, booked revenue, margins, and what needs attention, while daily reminders chase payments, staff confirmations, and post-event surveys. It brings inquiry, planning, procurement, and profitability into one connected system.

  • Is this app compatible with Odoo Enterprise?
    • Yes, our app is fully compatible with both Odoo Enterprise and Odoo Community editions.
  • How do date-relative checklists work?
    • Checklist deadlines are automatically computed relative to the event date (e.g. D-30, D-7). If the event date is rescheduled, all incomplete checklist deadlines automatically adjust accordingly.
  • Can clients e-sign quotes and pay deposits online?
    • Yes, clients can log into the integrated customer portal, sign event quotations digitally, and pay booking deposits online via standard Odoo payment gateways.
